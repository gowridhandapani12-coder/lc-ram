## 1.3 contents

Labs: Error detective (sort 10 situations into systematic/random) · Collect & fit (weigh standard masses on a balance
with a hidden zero error; line of best fit, intercept = zero error) · Stopwatch timing (reaction-time scatter, running
mean, timing 10 swings) · Target practice (bias and spread vs accuracy and precision) · Percentage error (true vs
measured) · Read the scale (metre stick, vernier, micrometer; estimated error = ½ smallest division). 5 goals,
25 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 35 glossary terms, 6 trainer types, "systematic or random?" drill, 3 long questions.

## 2.5 contents

Labs: Ray tracer (drag a point object; rays obey i = r and meet behind the mirror) · Mirror writing (lateral inversion,
symmetric letters) · Collect & fit (you → image vs you → mirror, gradient 2; approach speed 2v) · Rotate the mirror
(reflected ray turns 2θ) · Full-length mirror (½ your height, bottom edge at ½ eye height) · Periscope (two mirrors at
45°). 5 goals, 25 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 34 glossary terms, 6 trainer types, "true or false?" drill,
3 long questions.

## 2.3 contents

Labs: Where does the light go? (reflected/absorbed/transmitted by material) · Collect & fit (spread of reflected rays vs
surface roughness) · Who can see it? (mirror vs paper, six observers) · Glass and water (partial reflection vs angle,
Schlick approximation) · Glossy or matt? · Luminous or not? (sorter). 5 goals, 24 flashcards, 16 quiz questions,
35 glossary terms, 4 trainer types, "diffuse, regular or both?" drill, 3 long questions.

## 2.4 contents

Labs: Name the parts (label 7 spots) · Ray tracer (i = r, angle between rays 2i) · Collect & fit (ray box experiment,
r against i, gradient 1) · Run it backwards (reversibility) · Two mirrors (i₂ = θ − i₁, corner reflector at 90°) ·
Hit the target (tilt the mirror; ray turns 2θ). 5 goals, 25 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 35 glossary terms,
4 trainer types, "true or false?" drill, 3 long questions.

## 4.5 contents
