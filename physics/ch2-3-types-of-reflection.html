    Top exam tips for types of reflection

    1. Define reflection as the bouncing of light off a surface.
    2. Diffuse = rough surface, light scattered in all directions. Regular = smooth surface, light reflected in one direction.
    3. Draw a parallel beam (three parallel rays) for both, with arrows: parallel out for regular, scattered for diffuse.
    4. Diffuse reflection still obeys the laws of reflection at each tiny bit of the surface.
    5. We see non-luminous objects because they reflect light diffusely into our eyes.
    6. Glass and water give partial (weak regular) reflection; most light passes through.
    7. Use everyday examples: mirror, page, glossy car, wet road, shop window.
