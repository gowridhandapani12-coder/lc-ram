## Chapter 1 contents

Sections: dimensional analysis · scalars · vectors · the i–j plane · dot products · writing vectors in i and j ·
polar form · displacement and distance. Labs: Collect & fit (pendulum: T² against l, g from the gradient) · Speed vs
velocity (roundabout) · Tip to tail · Add, subtract, scale · Dot product (with projection) · Resolve it · Polar form
(quadrants) · Round the track. 8 goals, 25 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 35 glossary terms, 6 trainer types,
"scalar or vector?" drill, 3 long questions.

## Chapter 2 contents

Sections: uniform acceleration · time–velocity graphs · linear-equation problems · simultaneous equations ·
acceleration and deceleration · two objects · motion under gravity · letters instead of numbers. Labs: The equations
(motion strip) · Collect & fit (v against t: gradient a, intercept u, area s) · Which equation? (suvat wheel) · Two
equations, two unknowns ((u, a) plane) · Speed up, slow down · Catch up · Thrown upwards · Test a general result
(formula vs 0.001 s simulation). 8 goals, 24 flashcards, 16 quiz questions, 35 glossary terms, 7 trainer types,
"which equation?" drill, 3 long questions.

## Chapter 3 contents

Sections: why study projectiles · launched vertically · fired at an angle · speed and angle known · maximum range.
Labs: Dropped vs thrown (strobe) · Up meets down · Fired at an angle (r and v at time t) · Collect & fit (R against
u², g from the gradient) · Best angle (range vs angle, target angles). 5 goals, 24 flashcards, 16 quiz questions,
35 glossary terms, 6 trainer types, "true or false?" drill, 3 long questions.
