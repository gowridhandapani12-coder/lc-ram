Chapter 4 · Lenses · 4.5

One formula,
a minus sign,
and the image is found.

The lens formula 1/f = 1/u + 1/v works for a diverging lens too, as long as you get the signs right. Learn the "real is positive" rule, why a diverging lens always gives a small, upright, virtual image, and how to measure its focal length.

    Top exam tips for the diverging lens formula

    1. Write the formula first: 1/f = 1/u + 1/v.
    2. Diverging lens: put f in as negative. Forgetting this is the most common mistake.
    3. A negative v means a virtual image (on the same side as the object). Say so in your answer.
    4. Combine fractions carefully, e.g. 1/v = 1/f − 1/u, then flip the result.
    5. Magnification m = v ÷ u (use sizes); image height = m × object height.
    6. Check your answer: |v| must be smaller than |f| and smaller than u.
    7. Keep units consistent (all cm or all m).
    8. The questions here are practice questions, not official SEC papers.
