    Collect & fit

    Dimensional analysis says a pendulum's period must be T = k√(l/g). Test it: time a pendulum at different lengths (each timing has a small error), plot T² against l, and use the gradient to find g.

    Speed vs velocity

    A car drives round a roundabout at a constant 12 m/s. Its speed (a scalar) never changes. Its velocity (a vector) changes all the time, because its direction does.

    1Move the car. Which readout never changes?
    2At which positions is the velocity 12j? And −12i?
    3Is the car accelerating? Explain using vectors.
    Tip to tail

    Add two vectors by placing the tail of b on the tip of a. The resultant goes from the start to the finish.

    1Make a and b point the same way. What is |a + b|?
    2Make them opposite. What now?
    3At 90°, check |a + b| with Pythagoras.
    Add, subtract, scale

    In the i–j plane, work component by component: add the i parts, add the j parts. Choose the operation and watch the result.

    Show
    1Work out a + b by hand, then check.
    2For a − b, where does the arrow go from and to?
    3Find a and b so that a + b = 0.
    Dot product

    a·b = a₁b₁ + a₂b₂ = |a||b| cos θ. Turn b and watch the dot product change sign. When it's zero, the vectors are perpendicular.

    1Find two positions where a·b = 0.
    2When is a·b biggest? When is it most negative?
    3Check that both formulas give the same number.
    Resolve it

    A vector of size r at angle θ to the i direction splits into r cos θ i + r sin θ j. Change the vector and see the right-angled triangle.

    1Set θ = 30°, r = 20. Check the components with your calculator.
    2For which angles is the j component negative?
    3Find the unit vector in the same direction.
    Polar form

    Turn xi + yj into polar form r∠θ: r = √(x² + y²), and θ measured anticlockwise from the i direction. Watch the quadrant!

    1For −3i + 4j, what does tan⁻¹(4 ÷ −3) give on your calculator? Why is it wrong?
    2Find θ for each quadrant by drawing a sketch first.
    3Convert 10∠210° back to i and j.
    Round the track

    A runner goes round a circular 400 m track. Distance is how far they've run. Displacement is the straight line from the start to where they are now.

    1Where is the displacement biggest? How big is it?
    2After one full lap, what are the distance and displacement?
    3Can displacement ever be bigger than distance?

    Top exam tips for units and vectors

    1. Write dimensions in square brackets: [v] = LT⁻¹. Both sides of a correct equation must match.
    2. Numbers like ½ and 2π have no dimensions, so dimensional analysis can't find them.
    3. Mark vectors clearly: bold, an arrow on top, or a line underneath.
    4. |xi + yj| = √(x² + y²). Don't forget to square negative components properly.
    5. For polar form, sketch the vector first to see its quadrant, then find θ from the i axis.
    6. a·b is a number, not a vector. a·b = 0 means perpendicular (if neither is zero).
    7. Displacement is a vector from start to finish; distance is the total path length.
